Welcome to the Persian language.

Persian (also called Farsi) is a rich, poetic language spoken by tens of millions of people in Iran, Afghanistan and Tajikistan. This site is a gentle place to begin: learn the letters, read real sentences, rebuild them word by word, and hear how people actually talk.
