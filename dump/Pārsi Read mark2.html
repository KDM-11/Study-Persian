Welcome to the Persian language.

Persian (also called Farsi) is a rich, poetic language spoken by tens of millions of people in Iran, Afghanistan and Tajikistan. This site is a gentle place to begin: learn the letters, read real sentences, rebuild them word by word, and hear how people actually talk.

Reading practice

Read short, real Persian sentences one at a time. Transliteration and English translation can be switched on or off, so lean on them at first and drop them as you improve.

  1. Read the Persian line first.
  2. Turn on transliteration to check the pronunciation.
  3. Tap New text for another sentence.

The alphabet

Persian is written right to left in a script of 32 letters. Learn to recognise each letter and its sound before anything else.

  1. Look at each letter with its name and sound.
  2. Switch on Quiz mode to hide the answers.
  3. Tap a letter to check yourself.

ترتیب
tartib · order

Scramble

A word-order puzzle. You see an English sentence and a jumble of Persian words, then rebuild the sentence. Persian puts the verb at the end, so this trains how sentences are put together.

  1. Read the English sentence.
  2. Tap the Persian words in the right order.
  3. Press Check, or Hint if you are stuck.

Let's go colloquial

Written Persian and the way people really talk can be quite different. For example, می‌خواهم (mi-khāham) becomes می‌خوام (mi-khām) in speech. This section teaches the spoken form.

  1. Read the spoken phrase and its transliteration.
  2. Switch on the formal form to compare.
  3. Tap New phrase for another.
