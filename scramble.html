Scramble the sentence.

A word-order puzzle. You see an English sentence and a jumble of Persian words, then rebuild the sentence. Persian puts the verb at the end, so this trains how sentences are put together.

  1. Choose a level below.
  2. Read the English sentence.
  3. Tap the Persian words in order, or drag them to rearrange.
  4. Press Check, or Hint if you are stuck.
